The other two modes
Same picker, different command.
$ orion queue add OR-273 OR-275 OR-267 $ orion watch orion --max-jobs 2 --interval 60
queue add takes keys and inclusive ranges (OR-100 OR-140..OR-145) and leaves the watcher to pick them up. watch runs the queue by itself until stopped — the one mode that keeps spending after you close the tab, which is why it states the job and interval limits on the button.
