### Removed

- **The live watch display is gone; `orion watch` prints a plain sequential log.** The
  pinned region -- ticket rows redrawn four times a second, a frozen scrollback window,
  a batch block with a chain and a job list -- produced six defects of its own in two
  days (OR-307, OR-308, OR-313, OR-317, OR-319, OR-330), each costing a release and a
  watch run to find, and not one of them was about whether Orion did its work correctly.
  A log cannot strand a frame, miscount an erase, or hide a batch behind a region that
  failed to draw.

  **Nothing else changed.** Fan-out, subagents, the parallel coding queue, the batch
  integration queue, the QA suite runner and the CI fix loop are untouched: none of them
  lived in the display. Every fact the region showed is still printed, by the same
  packages, as it happens -- the region never had a source of its own.

  `orion watch --demo` is removed with it.
