### Changed

- The `orion watch` full-screen view is redesigned. Colour now means state:
  every section label is one slate chip and only NEEDS YOU keeps magenta.
  The header sums up the watch (running, queued, landed, failed and the spend)
  with the clock at the right. RUNNING is an aligned table of ticket, stage,
  elapsed time, who and what it is doing. A fan-out folds to a progress bar
  in its row and shows its tree only when a child fails. The bottom panel
  shows how long each batch step took, and CI's progress against its usual
  time. Set `ORION_THEME=light` for a light terminal or `ORION_THEME=mono` for
  no colour (`NO_COLOR` implies mono). The waiting icon is now `◷`, one cell
  wide on every terminal.
