### Fixed

- **`orion watch KEY` lands only its own project's work, and a project's
  batch is never built from another project's config.** The watcher limited
  what it started to the projects it was given, but reconciled every
  registered project's tickets waiting on CI. When that pass mixed two
  projects, the batch used the first project's workspace and config and the
  second project's tickets were skipped entirely — `orion watch LTA` spent its
  passes on a Continuity batch and never landed an LTA ticket. The watcher now
  collects only its own projects, and a pass that spans several (`orion
  collect`, or `orion watch` with no key) is reconciled one project at a time.
