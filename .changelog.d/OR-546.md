### Changed

- **A project created by `orion plan` starts with the CI fix loop on**
  (`ci.auto_fix`). A ticket a batch convicts is then fixed on its own branch —
  bounded at three attempts, stopped early by a repeated failure — instead of
  going to `orion-failed` and being re-run from scratch. An adopted repository
  keeps the default (off), since its suite was not written by Orion and may be
  flaky; an `orion.json` that already exists keeps its own setting.
