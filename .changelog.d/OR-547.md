### Fixed

- **Stopping `orion watch` says what it is waiting for, and a forced stop
  cleans up after itself.** The first ctrl-c now names each running ticket
  with its stage and how long it has run, prints a line every minute while it
  waits, and says "stopped" only once the last agent has finished — it used
  to say "stopped" immediately and then wait in silence, sometimes for hours.
  A second ctrl-c still kills the agents, and now also puts their tickets
  back in the queue (removes `orion-working` and the stage label) instead of
  leaving them claimed for a person to clear. The tracker gets ten seconds;
  any ticket it could not release is named.
