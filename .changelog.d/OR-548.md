### Changed

- **`orion watch` on a terminal is now a full-screen view, like `top`.** The
  board stays at the top and the newest log lines fill the rest, redrawn once
  a second, instead of the board being printed into the scroll every few
  minutes. Because the full-screen view has no scrollback, every line is also
  written to `~/.orion/logs/watch-<time>.log`, and the header shows the path.
  On exit the normal screen comes back with the last 30 lines on it. Use
  `--plain` to keep the scrolling log; a pipe, `--once` and `--dry-run` keep
  it automatically.

### Fixed

- A batch that went green after a watcher restart no longer shows "CI ◐" on
  the board while it lands.
